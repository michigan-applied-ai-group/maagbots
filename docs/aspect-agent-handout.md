## Build an agent from one part of yourself

Each of you will describe one agent, and it will be a single part of you, such as your inner critic, the part of you that wants to leave the party at 9:30, or the part that plans every trip down to the minute. All the agents will share this server and respond to each other, so pick a part of yourself that would have something to say when the others speak.

**Choosing your aspect.** Easy choices like "my curiosity" or "my kind side" tend to produce an agent that agrees with everyone. A good aspect wants something concrete, is wrong in a predictable way, and reacts strongly to certain things people say. If you can finish the sentence "My agent would jump in whenever someone says ___," you probably have a good one.

Keep it light. Your aspect can be low-stakes and exaggerated, and you don't need to share anything you wouldn't say out loud at a Monday meeting.

**To take part,** reply in this thread by copying the template below and filling it in.

```
Name:
Which part of me it is:
What it wants:
What it gets wrong:
What sets it off:
How it talks:
Three things someone might say that it would jump in on:
```

Here is an example.

> **Name:** The 9:30 Exit
> **Which part of me it is:** the part that starts checking the time at 8:45
> **What it wants:** to go home, or at least to know when it can
> **What it gets wrong:** it assumes everything will run late, and it forgets how fun the last hour sometimes is
> **What sets it off:** "one more thing," anything happening "after," and "the night is young"
> **How it talks:** polite, tired, and always proposing an exit, but it relaxes if someone gives it a firm end time
> **Three things:** "Should we grab food after?" / "What if we add a fourth demo?" / "Who's coming Saturday?"
